AI + AUTOMOTIVE · MULTIMODAL AGENT · PHYSICAL AI CONCEPT

DriveMate

A multi-agent mobility and vehicle-status assistant for urban commuters, family travelers, and long-distance drivers. It organizes fragmented schedule, vehicle, charging, and environmental information into explainable recommendations that remain under user control.

JTBD

Turn complex pre-trip constraints into an executable plan

When a user presents a compound task spanning schedule, weather, route, and vehicle status, the system consolidates facts, explains risks, and requests confirmation before key actions.

MVP Boundary

Validate understanding and planning—not vehicle control

The MVP covers information synthesis, vehicle-status explanation, route and charging suggestions, and reminder drafts. It does not control a vehicle or replace professional diagnosis.

    KEY PRODUCT DECISIONS

    Define constraints before deciding the level of automation

    Decision 01

    One orchestrator owns the explanation

    Specialist agents return local conclusions; the orchestrator resolves conflicts and presents sources and one user-facing plan.

    Decision 02

    Separate facts from inference

    Sensor or tool results carry timestamps. Model inference uses explicit qualifiers such as “may” and “recommend.”

    Decision 03

    Confirmation strength follows risk

    Information lookup needs no confirmation; reminder creation needs one confirmation; vehicle control is outside the MVP.

    Decision 04

    Degrade safely on exceptions

    When data is missing, the system states the gap and offers a human or professional path instead of filling in facts.

    Decision 05

    Multimodality must identify constraints

    Vision enters the decision chain only when it can contribute road, passenger, or risk facts—not as a demonstration gimmick.

    SAFETY & EVALUATION

    Constrain task completion with high-risk error measures

    Safety boundary

    • No direct execution of high-risk vehicle control
    • User confirmation for key actions, with cancellation available
    • Clear separation of facts, stale data, and model inference
    • Vehicle exceptions prioritize stopping safely or contacting professional support
    • Unavailable tools produce a stated information gap

    Planned concept metrics

    Task completionTool-call accuracyFactual consistencyConfirmation countResponse timeUser satisfactionHigh-risk error rate

    These are proposed evaluation metrics, not results from a deployed product.

    PHYSICAL AI / VLA ROADMAP

    From a static concept to a perception-language-action loop in controlled environments

    Current status: Stage 0. The work includes a product concept, information architecture, and static low-fidelity prototype only. No VLA model has been trained or integrated, and no real vehicle is connected.

    STAGE 0 · CURRENT

    Static concept validation

    Use synthetic scenarios to examine user tasks, information structure, safety boundaries, and confirmation policy.

    STAGE 1

    Read-only data sandbox

    Use simulated or authorized test data for vehicle state and map tools to evaluate factual consistency and tool calls.

    STAGE 2

    Multimodal risk understanding

    Evaluate vision-language recognition and explanation of flooding, obstacles, and cabin risk on offline labeled data.

    STAGE 3

    Controlled low-risk action

    Evaluate low-risk actions only in closed environments with explicit authorization, rule guardrails, human confirmation, and full logs.
